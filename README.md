# My Favorite Series

## Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is one of my favorite series because it combines mystery, science fiction, friendship, and adventure.

### Why I Like It

- Interesting characters
- Exciting storyline
- Mystery and suspense
- Great friendships
- Creative setting

### My Favorite Character

**Eleven** is my favorite character because she is brave, powerful, and cares about her friends.

### Rating

**9/10**

> "Friends don't lie."

I would recommend this series to people who enjoy **science fiction, mystery, and adventure**.
